= Perfect Rice Pilaf

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2&#8532; cups | low sodium chicken broth
| &#8531; cup | butter (unsalted)
| 1 tsp | kosher sea salt
| &#189; tsp | garlic powder
| &#187; tsp | ground black pepper
| &#8539; tsp | paprika
| 1 pinch | onion powder
| &#8531; cup | vermicelli or orzo
| 1 cup | long grain rice
| 1 tsp | fresh parsley (chopped)
|===

== Method

. Heat chick broth, butter, salt, garlic powder, paprika, and onion powder over medium heat. Reduce to low heat when it starts simmering.

. In a large skillet heat olive oil over low medium heat.

. When oil is hot add pasta and cook until brown (4 -6 minutes).

. Add rice and cook until it turns bright white (5 - 7 minutes).

. Pour warm broth into skillet and stir to mix. Bring to simmer, cover, and cook for 15 minutes.

. Remove cover and cook until all broth is gone (5 minutes).
