= Dijon mustard

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| &#188; cup | brown mustard seeds
| &#188; cup | yellow mustard seeds
| &#189; cup | dry white winefootnote:[Chardonnay or Pinot Grigio]
| &#189; cup | white wine vinegar
| &#189; tsp | salt
| 1 tsp | light brown sugar or honey (optional)
| 2 to 3 tbsp | water (if needed for thinning)
|===

== Method

.  Place the brown mustard seeds, yellow mustard seeds, white wine, and white wine vinegar into a small glass bowl or a clean jar.

.  Cover the container tightly and let the mixture sit at room temperature for 24 to 48 hours. This softens the seeds and releases their signature spicy kick.

.  Transfer the soaked seed and liquid mixture into a blender or food processor.

. Add the salt and optional sugar or honey. Blend until smooth, adding a tablespoon of water at a time if the mixture is too thick.

. Spoon the mustard into a clean glass jar with a tight lid and store it in the refrigerator. Let it rest for at least 24 hours (or up to 2 weeks) before using so the sharp heat can mellow into a balanced flavour.
