= Scones

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 3 cups | plain flour
| 4&#189; tsps | baking powder
| &#190; tsp | salt
| 80 g | unsalted butter
| 1 cup | cold milk
|===

== Method

. Preheat oven to 200&#176;C.

. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.

. Mix butter into flour mix until it looks like breadcrumbs.

. Pour milk all over the milk.

. Mix until breadcrumb texture changes to balls.

. Knead dough 10 times.

. Roll out and cut into 2 cm disks.

. Place on baking tray and brush tops with milk.

. Bake in oven for 10 - 12 mins.
