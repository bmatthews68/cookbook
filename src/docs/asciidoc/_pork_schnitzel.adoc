= Pork Schnitzel

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 4 | pork boneless chops/steaks (125 - 150 g each)
| &#190; tsp | salt
| &#189; tsp | black pepper
| 50 g | plain flour
| 2 | large eggs (whisked)
| 90 g | panko breadcrumbs
| 3 tbsps | xref:_herbes_de_provence.adoc#_herbes_de_provence[herbes de provence]
| 125 ml | vegetable oil
|===

== Method

. Pound each steak to 0.5 cm thickness.

. Mix flour, pepper, and pepper in first bowl.

. Whisk eggs in second bowl.

. Mix breadcrumbs and herbes de provence in third bowl.

. Heat oil in pan.

. Pre-heat oven to 70&#176;C.

. Coat each steak in flour, dip in eggs and cover with breadcrumbs.

. Fry on each side for 2 minutes and transfer to oven.
