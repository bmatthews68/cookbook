= Oatmeal Cookies

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 208 g | oatmeal flakes
| 240 g | granulated sugar
| 2     | large eggs
| 16 g  | baking powder
| pinch | salt (small)
| 272 g | assorted flour
| 80 g  | dried nuts / candied fruit
| 320 g | butter
|===

== Method

. Preheat oven to 200&#176;C.

. Mix all ingredients together.

. Cut cookies to size and place on baking tray in refrigerator to chill for 30 minutes.

. Bake in oven for 12-15 minutes.
