= Pork Loin Crock Pot

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1&#189; - 2&#188; kg | pork loinfootnote:[Not tenderloin] (trimmed of excess fat)
| 4 tbsps | butter slicked into 4 pads
2+a|
[.discrete]
=== Brine
| 6 cups | warm water
| &#189; cup | kosher salt (or &#188; cup table salt)
| &#189; cup | brown sugar
| &#188; cup | apple cider vinegar
| 2 | bay leaves
| 1 cup | ice cubes
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Spice Mix
| 1 tbsp | chilli powder
| 1 tbsp | garlic powder
| 1 tsp | onion powder
| 1 tsp | paprika
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#189; tsp | ground black pepper
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Honey Citrus Sauce
| &#189; cup | honey
| &#188; cup | apricot preserves
| 3 tbsps | orange juice
| 2 tbsps | balsamic vinegar
| 1 tbsp | soy sauce
| 1 tbsp | Dijon mustard
| 1 tsp | dried parslet
| 1 tsp | dried dried basil
| 1 tsp | dried rosemary
| &#189; | dried oregano
| &#189; | dried thyme
| &#188; | red pepper flakes
|===

== Method

. Mix brine solution in a large container with a whisk until everything has dissolved. Sub-merge pork and refridgerate for 90 minutes (or up to 24 hours).

. Remove pork and pat dry with paper towels.

. Whisk together all the spice mix ingredients in a small bowl and rub evenly over the pork.

. Lightly grease the slow cooker with butter.

. Add all the sauce ingredients to slow cooker and whisk to combine.

. Heat 2 tbsps of oil in a large skillet over medium high heat. When oil is just smoking add seasoned pork loin and sear each side until golden.

. Transfer pork loin to slow cooker. Spoon sauce over the pork loin and top with the 4 slabs of butter.

. Cook on low for 2 - 4 hours until the pork reaches between 63&#176;C and 64&#176;C. Check early and often!

. Remove pork and let it rest.

. Transfer sauce to a sauce pan. Whisk cornstarch and water add to the sauce pan. Simmer over a medium heat until thickened.
