= Elenas Pancakes

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 litre | milk
| 4 | large eggs
| 3 tbsps | sugar
| &#189; tsp | salt
| | flour
| 4 tbsp | sunflower oil
|===

== Method

. Mix milk, egs, sugar, salt, and flour with a whisk. The amount of flour required will depend on your desired consistency.

. Add sunflower oil before cooking.
