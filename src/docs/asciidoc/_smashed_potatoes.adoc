= Smashed Potatoes

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 kg | baby potatoes
| 4 tbsp | olive oil
| 3 cloves | garlic (minced)
| &#190; tsp | salt
| &#190; tsp | black pepper
| &#189; tsp | garlic salt
| &#189; tsp | smoked paprika
| 1&#189; tsp | parsley
|===

== Method

. Procedure place potatoes in salted cold water. Bring to boil and simmer for 15 - 20 minutes. Drain.

. Brush baking tray with 1 tbsp of olive oil. Baby potatoes should be placed on baking tray and flatten gently.

. Mix 3 tbsp of olive oil with garlic, salt, black pepper, garlic salt, and smoked paprika. Brush the flattened baby potatoes with the mixture.

. Bake in a pre-heated oven at 200&#176; for 20 - 25 minutes.

Garnish with parsley
