= Italian Seasoning

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 tbsp | dried basil
| 1 tbsp | dried thyme
| 1 tbsp | dried rosemary
| 1 tbsp | dried marjoram
| 1 tbsp | dried parsley
| 2 tbsp | dried oregano
| 1 tsp | red pepper flakes (optional)
| 1 tsp | garlic powder (optional)
|===


== Method

. Mix in small bowl.

. Transfer to jar and seal.
