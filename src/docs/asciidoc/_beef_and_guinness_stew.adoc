= Beef and Guinness Stew

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 3 tbsps | olive oil
| 1.7 kg | beef (e.g. beef chuck, boneless short rib, etc.)
| 1 tsp | salt
| 1 tsp | black pepper
| 4 cloves | garlic
| 3  | onions (chopped)
| 240 g | bacon (speck or pancetta) (diced)
| 4 tbsps | plain white flour
| 587 ml | Guinness (1 can)
| 6; tbsps | tomato paste
| 4 cups | chick stock / broth
| 4 | carrots (1&#188; cm chunks)
| 3 | celery stalks
| 2 | bay leaves
| 4 sprigs | thyme
|===

== Method

. Cut beef into chunks, pat dry, and sprinkle with salt and pepper.

. Heat oil in heavy pan and brown the beef in batches.

. Lower heat to medium and cook onions and garlic for 3 minutes.

. Add bacon to pan cook until brown.

. Stir in celery and carrot.

. Add flour and cook for 1 minute.

. Add Guinness, chicken stock / broth, and tomato paste, mixing well until the flour dissolves.

. Add bay leaves and thyme.

. Transfer contents of pan and beef to slow cooker.

. Cook on low for 7 - 8 hours.
