= Scrambled Eggs

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 4 | large eggs
| &#8539; tsp | salt
| &#188; tsp | paprika
| 4 tsps | butter (unsalted)
|===

== Method

. Whisk eggs, salt, and paprika until completely combined and foamy.

. Melt butter in skillet over low heat until bubbling.

. Add whisked eggs, stirring frequently for 2 -3 mins.

. Remove from heat to prevent drying.
