= Slow Cooker Italian Meatballs

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Sauce
| 795 g | whole plum tomatoes (2 cans)
| 170 g | tomato paste
| 3 tbsp | extra virgin olive oil
| 1 tsp | dried oregano
| &#188; tsp | crushed red chilli flakes
| 3 cloves | garlic (crushed)
| 2 | bay leaves
| 2 tsp | kosher salt
| 500 ml | beef stock
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Meatballs
| 1 | small onion (chunked)
| 1 | small carrot (chunked)
| 1 | celery stalk (chucked)
| &#189; cup | parsley
| 2 cloves | garlic (crushed)
| 500 g | lean beef (minced)
| 500 g | pork (minced)
| 2 | large eggs
| &#190; cup | Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
| &#189; cup | Parmesan
| 1 tsp | kosher salt
|===

== Method

. Pour tomatoes into pot and crush by hand.

. Stir in tomato paste, olive, oregano, chilli flakes, garlic, bay leaves, and salt.

. Mix in 500 ml of beef stock.

. Add onion, carrot, celery, parsley, garlic, beef, pork, eggs, breadcrumbs, Parmesan and salt into larget bowl and mix by hand.

. Form 2 - 4 cm meat balls and add to sauce.

. Cook on low for 4 - 5 hours.
