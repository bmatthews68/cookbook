= Swedish Meatballs

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Meatballs
| 2 tbsp | olive oil
| &#189; cup | onion (finely diced)
| 2 cloves | garlic (minced)
| &#189; cup | Panko breadcrumbs
| &#188; cup | Parmesan cheese
| 1 | large egg (whisked)
| &#8531; cup | milk
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#188; tsp | dried oregano
| &#188; tsp | ground allspice
| &#188; tsp | ground nutmeg
| &#188; tsp | ground black pepper
| 350 g | beef (minced)
| 225 g | pork (minced)
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Sauce
| 1 cube | chicken bouillon
| 2 cups | beef broth
| 2 tsp | Worcestershire sauce xref:_tips.adoc#_substitutions[(*)]
| 1 tsp | Dijon mustard
| 1 tsp | dried parsley
| 4 tbsp | butter (unsalted)
| 4 tbsp | plain flour
| &#189; cup | sour cream
|===

== Method

. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in pan. Sauté onions for 4 minutes. Add minced garlic and cook for another 1 minute.

. In large bowl, combine breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, whisked egg, milk, cooled onion and garlic mix, salt, dried oregano, ground allspice, ground nutmeg, and ground black pepper.

. Gently incorporate the minced beef and pork.

. Roll into 3.5 cm balls. Chill for 15 minutes (or overnightfootnote:[Cover if chilling overnight.])

. Combine beef broth, chicken bouillon, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, and dried parsley in a large measuring cup or beaker.

. Heat 1 tsp olive oil over a medium - high heat.

. Brown meatballs in batches without overcrowding the pan.

. Drain excess oil. Melt butter and use spatula to scrape fond.

. Stir in the flour gradually to make a roux.

. Transfer to a Dutch oven and gradually mix beef broth mixture with the roux.

. Bring to a boil.

. Temper the sour cream by mixing &#188; cup of the beef broth and roux mix with the sour cream.

. Stir the tempered sour cream into the beef broth and roux mix over a low heat.

. Add meatballs , cover and cook for 10 - 15 minutes.
