= Mexican Chorizo

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 450 g | coarsely ground lean pork
| 170 g | coarsely ground pork fat
| 5 cloves | garlic (minced)
| 2 tbsp | ancho chile powder
| 1 tbsp | sweet paprika
| 1 tsp | smoked paprika
| 1&#189; tsp | salt
| 2 tsp | dried Mexican oregano
| &#189; tsp | freshly ground black pepper
| &#189; tsp | ground cumin
| &#189; tsp | ground coriander
| &#188; tsp | ground cinnamon
| &#188; tsp | ground cloves
| 60 ml | cider vinegar
|===

== Procedure

. Place meat and other ingredients in large bowl.

. Mix by hand to thoroughly combine the mixture.

. Place in colander over a bowl covered with plastic wrap.

. Refrigerate for 3 days.

. Daily squeeze out and discard excess moisture.

. Create 3 loaves of meat and place in freezer bags and store in freezer until required.
