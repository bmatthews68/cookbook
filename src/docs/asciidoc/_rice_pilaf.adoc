= Rice Pilaf

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2 cups | white rice
| 2 tsp | extra virgin olive oil
| &#189; cup | chopped green onions (or yellow)
| &#189; cup | chopped celery
| 4 cups | chicken or vegetable stock/broth
| 2 tsp | kosher salt
| &#188; tsp | black pepper
| &#8539; tsp | cayenne pepper
| &#189;cup | chopped parsley
|===

== Method

. Heat olive oil in sauce pan and brown the rice.

. Add onion and celery and cook until softened.

. Add salt, black pepper and cayenne pepper.

. Add stock/broth and cook until liquid gone.

. Fluff with fork and stir in parsley.
