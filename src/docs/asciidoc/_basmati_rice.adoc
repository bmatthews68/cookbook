= Basmati Rice

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 cup | Basmati rice
| 1&#189; cups | water
| &#188; tsp | salt
|===

== Method

. Place water, rice (unwashed) and a pinch of salt in sauce pan over medium heat.

. Bring to a simmer (uncovered).

. Cover with a lid and cook for 12 minutes.

. Check if water is gone after 12 minutes. If not replace lid quickly and cook for longer.

. Remove from heat and allow to steam (keep the lid on!) for 5 - 10 minutes.

. Fluff with a fork.
