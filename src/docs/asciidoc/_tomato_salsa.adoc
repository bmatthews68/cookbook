= Tomato Salsa

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 3 | tomatoes (chopped)
| &#189; cup | onion (finely diced)
| 2 - 3 | serrano chillis (finely chopped)
| &#189; cup | cilantro (chopped)
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#189; | lime (juice)
|===

== Method

. Mix ingredients in a bowl.

. Chill for 1 hour.
