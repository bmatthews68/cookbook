= Sandwich Bread

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 cup | warm waterfootnote:[43&#176;C] (240 ml)
| &#188; cup | warm milkfootnote:[43&#176;C] (60 ml)
| 2&#188; tsp | yeast (i.e. 1 standard 7g sachet)
| 2 tbsp | granulated sugar (25g)
| &#188; cup | unsalted butter (56 g)
| 1&#189; tsp | salt (9 g)
| 3&#8531; cups | plain flour (420 - 430 g)
|===

== Method

. Whisk water, milk, yeast and sugar in bowl. Cover and let sit for 5 minutes.

. Add butter, salt, and 1 cup of flour. Beat for slowly 30 seconds.

. Add 1 more cup of flour and beat more vigoursly until absorbed.

. Add remaining flour and beat until flour absorbed and butter lumps eliminated (about 2 minutes).

. Knead dough for 5 - 8 minutes. Dough should be soft (poke with finger, and it should push back into shape).

. Place dough in greased bowl and cover and let rise for 1 - 2 hours.

. Line bread tin with parchment paper and grease the parchment paper.

. Punch down dough to release air.

. Roll dough into large rectangular log to fit bread tin.

. Place dough in bread tin, cover, and let rise for 1 hour.

. Pre-heat oven to 180&#176;C.

. Bake for 32 - 36 minutes.
