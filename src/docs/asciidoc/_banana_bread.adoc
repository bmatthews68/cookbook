= Banana Bread

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Dry
| 2&#188; cups | plain flour
| 1 tsp | baking soda
| &#189; tsp | cooking salt
| 1&#188; tsps | cinnamon powder
| &#188; tsp | all spice
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Wet
| 4 | medium ripe bananas (400 g peeled)
| 1 cup | brown sugar
| 3 | large eggs (3 egg yolks, 2 egg whites)
| &#8531; cup | sour cream
| 80 g | butter (unsalted)
| &#8531; cup | vegetable oil
| 2&#189; tsps | honey
|===

== Method

. Preheat oven to 180&#176;C.

. Whisk dry ingredients.

. Mash bananas with potato masher in a separate bowl.

. Add wet ingredients to banana purée and whisk to combine.

. Add dry ingredients and mix with spatula until flour has just incorporated.

. Place in baking tin.

. Bake for 70 minutes.
