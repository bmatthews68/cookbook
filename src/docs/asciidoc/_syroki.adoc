= Syroki

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Filling
| 300 g | cottage cheese (~ 9% fat)
| 40 g | butter (unsalted)
| 40 g | powdered sugar
| 1 tsp | vanilla extract
| 2 tsps | cocoa powder
| &#8539; tsp | salt
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Glaze
| 80 g | dark chocolate
| 20 ml | neutral vegetable oil (or coconut oil)
|===

== Method

. Mix all ingredients for the filling except cocoa powder in a bowl.

. Blend into a creamy paste with immersion blender.

. Divide mixture 50/50 and add cocoa powder to one of the portions.

. Make 5 - 7 cm long bars.

. Refrigerate for 2 - 3 hours.

. Make glaze by melting chocolate with oil over low heat.

. Coat bars with glaze and return to refrigerator.
