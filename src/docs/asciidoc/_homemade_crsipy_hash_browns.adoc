= Homemade Crispy Hash Browns

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2 | medium Russet potatoes (shredded)
| &#189; | medium onion (finely chopped)
| &#188; cup | all-purpose flour
| 1 | large egg
| 1 cup | oil for frying
| &#189; tsp | salt
| &#188; tsp | black pepper
|===

== Method

. Rinse shredded potatoes until water runs clear. Squeeze and dry.

. Mix potato, onion, flour and egg in a bowl.

. Heat oil until sizzling on a medium high heat.

. Add mix to pan and cook for about 5 minutes on each side.

. Remove from pan and drain on paper towels.

. Season with salt and pepper.
