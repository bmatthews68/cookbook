= Pork and Beef Cutlets

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 700 g | beef (minced)
| 1 kg | pork (minced)
| 2 | medium onions (diced)
| 1 | large egg
| 1&#189; tsp | salt
| 1 tsp | black pepper
| &#188; tsp | nutmeg
| 1 cup | breadcrumbs
| | vegetable oil
|===

== Method

. Gently mix the meats, onions, egg, salt, pepper, nutmeg and breadcrumbs together in a large bowl. Don't overwork!

. Tear off uniform size chunks and roll into balls.

. Heat some vegetable oil on a hot pan.

. Place meat balls on pan and squash into cutlets using heel of palm.
