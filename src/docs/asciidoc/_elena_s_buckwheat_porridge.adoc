= Elena's Buckwheat Porridge

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 | chicken breast
| 2 cups | buckwheat porridge
| 6 cups | water
| 1 | chicken stock cube
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#189; tsp | black pepper
| &#189; | onion (diced)
| &#189; | carrot (diced)
|===

== Method

. Add chicken to pot.

. Cover with buckwheat.

. Add water, carrot, onion, salt, pepper, crumble stock cube, and bring to boil.

. After 10 minutes check. If water remains then cook a little longer.

. Rest for 10 minutes.
