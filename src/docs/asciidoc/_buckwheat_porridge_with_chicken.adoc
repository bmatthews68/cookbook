= Buckwheat Porridge with Chicken

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2 cups | buckwheat porridge
| 2 | chicken breats (diced)
| 1 tsp | salt
| 1 tbsp | butter (unsalted)
| 6 cups | water
|===

== Method

. Add chicken to pot.

. Cover with buckwheat.

. Add water and bring to boil.

. After 10 minutes check. If water remains then cook a little longer.

. Stir in salt and butter. Rest for 10 minutes.
