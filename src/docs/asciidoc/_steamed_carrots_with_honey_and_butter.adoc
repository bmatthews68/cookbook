= Creamed Carrots with Honey and Butter

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 500 g | carrots (halve length-wise and cut into 3 cm chunks)
| 2 tbsps | butter (unsalted)
| 1 tbsp | honey (clear)
| 1 tsp | lemon juice
| &#188; tsp | red chilli flakes
| &#189; tsp | kosher salt
| 1 tsp | lemon zest
|===

== Method


. Steam carrots for 15 - 25 minutes.

. Combine butter, honey, lemon juice, chilli flakes, and salt. Place in microwave for 15 seconds to melt the butter. Stir well.

. Place carrots in serving bowl. Add mixture and toss to coat the carrots.

. Sprinkle on lemon zest (and optionally flaky sea salt).
