= Baked Potato

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| | potatoes
| | olive oil
| | salt
|===

== Method

. Wash and scrub medium or large russet potatoes, then dry them completely.

. Use a fork to poke 6 to 8 holes all over each potato to let steam escape while baking.

. Rub the skin with olive oil or avocado oil and generously coat with sea salt or kosher salt to build a crispy crust. Skip the aluminium foil wrapper, which traps moisture and softens the skin.

. Place directly on a baking sheet or wire rack and bake at 20&#176;°C–220&#176;C for 45 to 60 minutes (or until the centre reaches about 96&#176;C–99&#176;C or is easily pierced with a fork/knife).

. Cut a slit down the centre, gently squeeze the ends inward to pop it open, and fluff the inside with a fork before adding toppings.
