= Creamed Spinach

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 570 g | baby spinach
| 2 tbsp | butter (unsalted)
| &#189; | medium yellow onion (finely chopped)
| 2 cloves | garlic (minced)
| &#189; cup | milk
| &#188; cup | heavy cream
| 114 g | cream cheese
| | salt (kosher)
| | ground black pepper
| &#188; tsp | cayenne pepper
| &#188; cup | Parmesan (grated)
|===

== Method

. Fill pot with water, add salt and bring to boil.

. Cook spinach for 30 seconds in boiling water.

. Drain spinach and place in ice water to cool.

. Drain ice water and squeeze out excess water.

. Melt butter in a large skillet over a medium heat.

. Add onion to skillet and cook until soft (about 5 minutes).

. Add garlic and cook until fragremnt (about 1 minute).

. Add milk, heavy cream, and cream cheese and allow to simmer until cream cheese has melted.

. Season with salt, ground black pepper, and cayenne pepper.

. Add spinach and Parmesan. Stir to combine.
