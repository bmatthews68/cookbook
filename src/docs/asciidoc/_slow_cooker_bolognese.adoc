= Slow Cooker Bolognese

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 4 tbsp | olive oil
| 4 stalks | celery (chopped)
| 2 | carrots (chopped)
| 2 | onions (chopped)
| | kosher salt and black pepper
| 6 cloves | garlic (minced)
| 170 g | tomato paste
| 1 cup | dry red wine
| 1.5 kg | beef (minced)
| 2 cups | whole milk
| 800 g | crushed tomatoes (2 cans)
| 1 tbsp | dried oregano
| 1 tbsp | dried basil
| 2 tsp | dried thyme
| 2 tsp | red pepper flakes
| &#189; tsp | nutmeg
| 1 | Parmesan rind (optional)
|===

== Method

. Heat 2 tbsp of olive oil in pan. Add celery, onions, and carrots and season with salt and pepper. Cook until softened (10 minutes).

. Stir in garlic for 30 seconds.

. Add tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.

. Add red wine and cook for 3 minutes.

. Transfer contents of pan to slow cooker.

. Heat remaining 2 tbsp of olive oil in pan and add minced beef. Season with salt and pepper. Cook for 15 minutes.

. Drain excess fat. Stir in milk. Cook for about 5 minutes until meat absorbs milk.

. Transfer to slow cooker.

. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, thyme, red pepper flakes, nutmeg, and optionally Parmesan rind. Add salt if required.

. Cook on low for 6 hours.

. Remove excess fat and add water if too thick. Cook for another 30 minutes.

Serve with spaghetti and grated Parmesan.
