= Chicken Plov

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2 cups | rice (long grain)
| 7 tbsps | vegetable oil
| 1 kg | chicken breastsfootnote:[4 chicken breasts] (diced)
| 2 | onions (sliced)
| 3 | carrots (julienned)
| 1 tsp | turmeric
| 1 tsp | black pepper
| 1 tsp | cumin
| 1 tsp | chilli flakes
| 1 tbsp | barberries
| 4 | bay leaves
| 1 tbsp | salt
| 3 heads | garlic
| 2 cups | chicken broth
|===

== Method

. Wet brine chicken breasts overnight.

. Rinse rice and leave it to soak.

. Heat vegetable oil and sauté onions on a high heat for 2 - 3 minutes.

. Add chicken and fry for 3 minutes until browned.

. Add carrots and fry for 2 - 3 minutes.

. Add turmeric, black pepper, cumin, chilli flakes, barberries, bay leaves, and salt and mix.

. Add 2 cups of chicken broth, stir, cover and allow to simmer for 10 minutes.

. Drain rice and layer it over the chicken. Add hot water if necessary.

. Insert garlic heads into rice. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes.

. If water remains poke holes in rice around the edge to allow it to evaporate while you cook for another 10 minutes.

. Mix well and serve.
