= Spanish Omelette

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 500 g | waxy potatoes (peeled and thin-sliced)
| 1 | red or green bell pepper (chopped)
| 1 | medium onion (sliced)
| 6 | large eggs
| &#189; cup | olive oil
| | salt (to taste)
| | black pepper (to taste)
|===

== Method

* Heat the olive oil in a non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat.

* Add the potatoes and onions.

* Cook for 15 minutes until soft.

* Add the chopped peppers.

* Cook for 5 more minutes until tender.

* Drain the vegetables well, leaving a little oil in the pan.

* Beat the eggs in a large bowl and season with salt and pepper.

* Stir the warm cooked vegetables into the egg mixture.

* Pour the mix back into the warm pan over medium heat.

* Cook for 4 minutes until the bottom sets.

* Place a large plate over the pan and flip the omelette onto the plate.

* Slide the omelette back into the pan to cook the other side for 3 more minutes.

* Cool slightly before slicing into wedges.
