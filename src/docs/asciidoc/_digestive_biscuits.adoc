= Digestive Biscuits

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 200 g | wholemeal flour
| 100 g | plain flour
| 100 g | porridge oats
| 300 g | butter (unsalted)
| 80 g | caster sugar
| &#190; tsp | baking soda
| &#190; tsp | salt
| 2 tbsp | milk
|===

== Method

. Mix flours (both), oats and butter in food processor. Blitz until it resembles fine breadcrumbs.

. Stir in sugar, baking soda, and salt.

. Pour in milk.

. Mix into a dough.

. Rollout to 5 mm thick and cut into biscuits with cookie cutter.

. Place biscuits on a baking tray and chill in refrigerator for 30 mins.

. Preheat oven to 200&#176;C.

. Bake cookies for 12 - 15 minutes in the oven.

. Leave on a tray to cool.
