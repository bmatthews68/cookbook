= Guacamole

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 - 4 | mild chillis (finely chopped)
| 1 bunch | coriander (chopped)
| 2 | tomatoes (finely chopped)
| | salt (to taste)
| 1 | onion (finely chopped)
| &#189; | lime (juice only)
| 3 | ripe avocados
|===

== Method

. Pound chillis, coriander, tomatoes, salt, and onion into a fine paste with pestle and mortar.

. Add lime just and a little water.

. Mash in the avocados.
