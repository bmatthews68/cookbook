= Syrniki

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 700 g | cottage cheese
| 7 tbsp | flour (heaped)
| 1 | large egg
| 3&#189; tbsp | sugar
| 1 tsp | butter (unsalted)
|===

== Method

. Mix cottage cheese, flour, egg and sugar in bowl.

. Form into balls and squash to flatten into 1 - 2 cm high patties.

. Melt 1 tsp of butter on pan.

. Cook patties on low heat browning each side.
