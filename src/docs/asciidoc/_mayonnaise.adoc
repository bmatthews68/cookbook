= Mayonnaise

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 | large egg
| 2 tsp | freshly squeezed lemon juice
| &#189; tsp | dried mustardfootnote:[Or 1 tsp Dijon mustard]
| &#189; tsp | kosher salt
| 1 cup | neutral oil, (canola, vegetable, or safflower)
|===

== Method

. Place 1 pasteurized large egg, 2 teaspoons lemon juice, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt in a tall jar (like a 3-cup Mason jar) or transparent container that is just a bit wider than the head of the immersion blender. (If you don’t have one, it’s best to use one of the other options for making mayonnaise.) Pour 1 cup neutral oil on top.

. Insert the immersion blender, making sure it is sitting against the bottom of the jar. Turn the blender on (use the low setting if available) but do not move or lift it. Blend until the bottom starts to look more mayonnaise-like, then slowly lift and tilt the blender up to blend in all the oil until thick and creamy.
