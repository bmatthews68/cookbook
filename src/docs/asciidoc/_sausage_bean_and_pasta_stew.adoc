= Sausage, bean and pasta stew

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 | onion (cut into 1cm pieces)
| 2 | carrots (finely chopped)
| 4 gloves | garlic (finely chopped)
| 225 g | dried white beans (e.g. cannellini)
| 6 - 8 sprigs | thyme
| 500 g | sweet Italian sausage
| 400 g | fire roasted tomatoes (1 can)
| 110 g | Parmesan rind (optional)
| 3 cups | low sodium chick broth or stock
| &#189; cup | ditalini pasta
| 2 tbsp | chopped parsley
| 2 tsp | balsamic vinegar
| | salt & pepper
|===

== Method

. Create layer of onions and top with carrots, garlic, white beans, thyme bundle, and sausages.

. Mix dried tomatoes, broth and 3 cups of water. Pour over sausages.

. Optionally add Parmesan rind.

. Cook on low for 7 - 8 hours.

. Fish out and discard thyme bundle and transfer sausages to board for slicing.

. Add pasta and cook for another 20 - 30 minutes.

. Turn off heat, slice sausages, and stir the sausages back into the stew.

Serve with grated Parmesan and crusty bread.
