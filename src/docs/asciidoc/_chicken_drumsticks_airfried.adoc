= Chicken Drumsticks (Air Fried)

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 5-6 | chicken drum sticks (bone-in)
| 1-1&#188; tbsp | olive oil
| 1 tsp | smoked paprika
| &#188; tsp | garlic powder
| &#189; tsp | onion powder
| &#188; tsp | dried oregano or Italian seasoning
| &#188; tsp | salt
| &#189;-&#188; tsp | black pepper (freshly cracked)
| 1/8 tsp | cayenne pepper (for heat)
| 1 tbsp | baking powder (for crispiness)
|===

== Method

. Pat the drumsticks completely dry with paper towels so the oil and spices stick to the skin.

.  Place the drumsticks in a large bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and toss to coat. In a separate small bowl, mix all your dry spices (and baking powder, if using), then sprinkle them evenly over the chicken, rubbing the spices in on all sides.

. Preheat your air fryer to 200&#176;C for about 5 minutes.

. Place the drumsticks in the air fryer basket in a single layer. Make sure they do not overlap or touch so hot air can circulate freely. Cook in batches if your basket is small.

. Air fry for 10 to 12 minutes on the first side.

. Use tongs to flip the drumsticks over.

. Continue air frying for another 8 to 10 minutes.

. Insert an instant-read meat thermometer into the thickest part of a drumstick (avoiding the bone). The internal temperature should reach at least 74&#176;C, though dark meat is often extra tender and juicy when cooked to 80&#176;C–82&#176;C.

. Remove the drumsticks from the air fryer and let them rest for 3 to 5 minutes before serving so the juices redistribute.
