= Pancakes

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1&#189; cups | plain flour
| 3&#189; tsps | baking powder
| 1 tbsp | sugar
| &#188; tsp | salt
| 1&#188; cups | milk
| 3 tbsps | butter (unsalted)
| 1 | large egg
|===

== Method

. Mix dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, sugar, salt).

. Make a well and add wet ingredients (milk, butter, egg).

. Mix dry ingredients into well until smooth batter is formed.
