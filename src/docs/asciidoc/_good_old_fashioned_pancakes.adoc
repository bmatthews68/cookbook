= Good Old-Fashioned Pancakes

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1&#189; cups | all-purpose flour
| 3&#189; tsps | baking powder
| 1 tbsp | white sugar
| &#188; tsp | salt
| 1&#189; cups | milk
| 3 tbsps | butter (melted)
| 1 | large egg
|===

== Method

. Mix sifted flour, baking soda, sugar and salt together in a large bowl.

. Make a well in the centre and add milk, melted butter, and whisked egg and mix until smooth.

. Heat lightly oiled griddle or pan over medium-high heat.

. Pour &#188; cup of batter onto gridle for each pancake and cook for 2 - 3 minutes per side.
