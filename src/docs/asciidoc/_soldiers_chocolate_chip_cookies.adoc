= Soldier's Chocolate Chip Cookies

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 225 g | butter (softened)
| 110 g | caster sugar
| 275 g | plain flour
| 1 tsp | cinnamon powder
| 1 tsp | baking powder
| 75 g | white or milk chocolate chips, or caramel chunks
|===

== Method

. Whip butter in bowl until creamy.

. Whip in the caster sugar.

. Sift in the flour.

. Add cinnamon, baking powder, and chocolate chips.

. Mix by hand in figure 8 motion until dough is formed.

. Roll a chunk of dough flat and cut out biscuits with a cookie cutter.

. Place on parchment paper in backing tray.

. Bake in a pre-heated oven at 175&#176;C for 10 - 12 minutes.
