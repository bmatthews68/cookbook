= Tortillas

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Option 1 -- with water
| 2 cups | plain flour
| 3 tbsp | olive oil
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#190; cup | hot water
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Option 2 -- with greek yoghurt
| 260 g | plain flour
| 2 tbsp | olive oil
| 1 tsp | salt
| 1&#189; tsp | baking powder
| 200 g | greek yoghurt
|===

== Method

. Mix ingredients in bowl and form into dough.

. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.

. Divide and from smaller dough balls.

. Roll dough balls thin and cook for 30 - 60 seconds per side on hot pan.
