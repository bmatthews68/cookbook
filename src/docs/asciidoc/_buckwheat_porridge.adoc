= Buckwheat Porridge

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 cup | roasted buckwheat groats
| 3 cups | water
| 1 tbsp | butter (unsalted)
| &#189; tsp | salt
|===

== Method

. Wash buckwheat groats

. Bring water to boil

. Add buckwheat groats and cook covered for 10 minutes.

. Turn off heat. Add salt and let sit for 10 minutes.

. Top with butter.
