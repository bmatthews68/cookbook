= French Toast

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| &#190; cup | milk
| 2 | large eggs
| 1 tsp | vanilla extract (optional)
| &#188; tsp | ground cinnamon or nutmeg (optional)
| | salt to taste
| 6 | thick slices of bread
| 1 tbsp | butter (unsalted)
|===

== Method

. Whisk eggs, milk, salt and optional spices together in a shallow bowl.

. Lightly butter skillet and heat over medium - high heat.

. Dunk bread slices in mixture, soak both sides.

. Transfer bread to skillet, cook until golden (3 - 4 minutes) per side.
