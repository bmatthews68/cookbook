= Tahini

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 cup | sesame hulled sesame seeds (140 g)
| 4 tbsps | sesame (not toasted), avocado, grape seed, vegetable or light olive oil
| &#189; tsp | salt
|===

== Method

. Toast sesame seeds in pan on a medium / low heat for 3 -5 minutes.

. Grind into paste for 2 minutes in food processor.

. Add oil and process for another 2 - 4 minutes.

. Add salt and process for 10 seconds.
