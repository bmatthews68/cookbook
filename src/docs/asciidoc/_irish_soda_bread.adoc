= Irish Soda Bread

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Dough
| 4 cups | plain flour
| &#189; cup | unsalted butter (softened)
| 4 tbsp | white sugar
| 1 tsp | baking soda
| 1 tbsp | baking powder
| &#189; tsp | salt
| 1 cup | buttermilkfootnote:[Use kefir in Russia]
| 1 | large egg
2+a|
[discrete]
| &#188; cup | butter milk
| &#188; cup | unsalted butter (melted)
=== Glaze

|===

== Method

. Preheat oven to 190&#176;C.

. Lightly grease baking sheet.

. Mix flour, softened butter, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in large bowl.

. Stir in butter milk and egg.

. Turn mixture out onto a lightly floured surface and knead slightly.

. Form into a rounded loaf place on baking tray.

. Combine &#188; cup melted butter and &#188; cup butter milk to make glaze.

. Cut a large X into the top of the loaf and brush on glaze.

. Place in oven for 45 - 50 minutes. But check if done from 30 minutes by inserting toothpick and checking if it comes out clean.

. Re-apply glaze whenever you take it out of the oven.

. Remove from oven and place on a rack to cool.
