= Slow Cooker Sheppard's Pie

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 900 g | beef (minced)
| 1 cup | onion (chopped)
| 3 cloves | garlic (minced)
| 1&#189; cup / 340 g | frozen vegetable mix (carrots and peas)
| 1&#189; cup / 350 ml | beef broth
| 60 g | tomato paste
| 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce xref:_tips.adoc#_substitutions[(*)]
| &#189; tbsp | cornstarch
| 1 tsp; | xref:_italian_seasoning.adoc#_italian_seasoning[Italian seasoning]
| &#189; tsp | salt
| &#189; tsp | black pepper
| 680 g | potatoes (mashed)
| 1 cup | cheddar cheese (shredded)
| | diced paprika and/or freshly chopped parsley for garnish
|===

== Method

. Cook minced beef and onion over a medium heat for 8 minutes.

. Stir in garlic and cook for another 1 minute.

. Drain fat and transfer beef to slow cooker and add frozen vegetables.

. Whisk beef broth, tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, corn starch, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Pour over the beef and frozen vegetables.

. Cook on low for 4 - 6 hours.

. Stir cheese into mashed potatoes, cover beef, frozen vegetables, and broth mix with a layer of mashed potatoes. Then cook for another 20 - 30 minutes.
