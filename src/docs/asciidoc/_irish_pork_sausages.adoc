= Irish pork sausages

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 kg | pork
| 1 | egg (large)
| 1 tsp | salt
| 1 tsp | white or black pepper
| &#189; tsp | dried sage
| &#189; tsp | dried thyme
| &#188; tsp | ground nutmeg
| &#188; tsp | ground ginger or all spice
| &#189; cup | finely minced onion or shallot
| 1 cup | breadcrumbs
|===

== Method

. Mince pork and onion together.

. Add other ingredients and mix thoroughly.

. Feed mixture back through the meat grinder with sausage attachment into natural sausage skins.
