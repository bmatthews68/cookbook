= Chicken Kiev

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Garlic Butter
| 12 tsp / 180 g | butter (unsalted)
| 4 tsp | freshly chopped parsley
| 4 cloves | garlic (minced)
| &#189; tsp | salt
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Chicken Kievs
| 4 | chicken breats (220 - 250 g each)
| 1 tsp | salt
| &#189; tsp | black pepper
| 2 | eggs (lightly whisked)
| &#189; cup | flour
| 2 cups | breadcrumbs
|===

== Method

. Mix garlic butter ingredients in a bowl. Roll up in parchment paper and chill in fridge.

. Cut breasts in half (horizontally) and flatten with a rolling pin if necessary.

. Place baton of butter in the middle of each flattened breast and roll the chicken around the butter.

. Freeze for 30 minutes wrapped in cling wrap.

. Bread chicken coating it with flour, dipping it in the egg and covering in breadcrumbs.

. Freeze for another 30 minutes.

. Cook in air fryer for 18 - 20 minutes at 180&#176;C - 200&#176;C.
