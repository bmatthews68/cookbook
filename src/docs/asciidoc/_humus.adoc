= Humus

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 425 g | chickpeas (can or jar)
| 1 | lemon (juiced)
| &#188; cup | tahini
| 1 clove | garlic (small)
| &#189; tsp | ground cumin
| 2 tbsps | extra virgin olive oil
| &#189; tsp | salt
| 2 - 3 tbsps | cold water or aquafaba
| | ground paprika for serving
|===

== Method

. Whip tahini and lemon juice in food processor for 1 minute. Scrape sides and whip for another 30 seconds.

. Add olive oil, garlic, cumin, and &#189; tsp salt and process for 30 seconds.  Scrape sides and whip for another 30 seconds.

. Add half the chickpeas and process for another 1 minute.

. Scrape sides, add rest of chickpeas and process for another 2 minutes.

. Add water / aquafabafootnote:[from jar of chickpeas] in increments and process until desired consistency is reached.

. Add more salt or lemon if required.

. Serve with olive oil and paprika.
