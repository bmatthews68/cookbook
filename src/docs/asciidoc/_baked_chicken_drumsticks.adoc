= Baked Chicken Drumsticks

== Ingredient

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 1 tsp | smoked paprika
| &#189; tsp | garlic powder
| &#188; tsp | onion powder
| &#8539; tsp | cayenne powder
| &#188; tsp | dried oregano
| &#189; tsp | salt
| &#188; tsp | black pepper
| 6 | chicken drum sticks
| 2 tbsp | cooking oil
|===

== Method

. Preheat oven to 220&#176;C.

. Mix smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne pepper, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper in bowl.

. Place chicken drum sticks in a bowl and drizzle with cooking oil, then sprinkle with seasoning. Toss until everything is evenly coated.

. Place seasoned drumsticks on parchment paper in baking tray.

. Bake for 40 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 80&#176;C. Flip after 20 minutes.
