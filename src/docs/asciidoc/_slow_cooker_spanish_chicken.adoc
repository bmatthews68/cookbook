= Slow Cooker Spanish Chicken

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
| 2 tbsps | olive oil
| 1 | Spanish onion (halved & sliced)
| 12 | chicken thighs (skinless)
| 225 g | chorizo picante (thinly sliced)
| 1 | red pepper (chunked)
| 1 | green pepper (chunked)
| 1 | yellow pepper (chunked)
| 150 g | Spanish pimento stuffed olives
| 300 ml | dry white wine
| 300 ml | chicken stock
| 1 tbsp | tomato purée
|===

== Method

. Heat olive oil in pan and fry Spanish onion until golden and then transfer to slow cooker.

. Fry chicken thighs with chorizo in batches until they start to colour and then transfer to slow cooker.

. Cut peppers into chunks and add to slow cooker.

. Add stuffed olives to slow cooker.

. Mix wine, chicken stock and tomato purée in pan. Heat and transfer to slow cooker scraping the fond from the pan.

. Cook on low for 6 - 8 hours.
