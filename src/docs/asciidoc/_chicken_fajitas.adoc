= Chicken Fajitas

== Ingredients

[%autowidth,frame=none,grid=none]
|===
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Seasoning
| &#189; tbsp | chilli powder
| &#189; tbsp | ground cumin
| 1 tsp | garlic powder
| &#189; tsp | paprika
| &#189; tsp | oregano
| &#189; tsp | kosher salt
| &#188; tsp | ground black pepper
2+a|
[discrete]
=== Fajitas
| 3 | boneless & skinless chicken breasts
| 1 | onion (thinly sliced)
| 1 | red bell pepper (thinly sliced)
| 1 | green bell pepper (thinly sliced)
| 1 | yellow bell pepper (thinly sliced)
| 2 tbsp | olive oil
| &#189; | lime (juiced)
|===

== Method

. Mix seasoning ingredients in a small bowl.

. Heat oil in pan.

. Season chicken breasts and sear the breasts for 7 - 8 minutes on each side.

. Slice onions and paprika

. Remove chicken from pan.

. Cook onion and paprika for 4 - 5 minutes.

. Slice chicken, add back to the pan, sprinkle lime juice and mix.

Server with xref:_tortillas.adoc#_tortillas[tortillas] and sour cream, xref:_guacamole.adoc#_guacamole[guacamole], and/or xref:_tomato_salsa.adoc#_tomato_salsa[salsa].
